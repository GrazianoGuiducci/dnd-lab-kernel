# Continuing competences and their guides

Research, monitoring, concept formation and competence evolution are initial
entrances into one field. Use them where the present work makes them pertinent.
They may develop further forms and combine with the receiving environment's
own capabilities. There is no fixed primary/support hierarchy.

For a continuing competence, retain its useful knowledge, reasons, applicability,
source contact, corrections and open possibilities in a guide. Keep a small
current margin that reaches the guide. The guide can change with experience;
the Lab's context and sources retain their owners.

The local support tool stores an open resultant exactly, and understands these
optional carriers when supplied by the cognitive receiver:

* `memory_effect: "RETAIN"` retains the resultant as a source-bound form.
* A `memory_effect` object with `action: "RETAIN"`, `knowledge` and optional
  `activation_ids` can retain the method or transformed recognition more
  precisely. Knowledge may carry reasons, conditions, questions and invalidators
  in whatever form actually occurred.
* `action: "COOL"` with `competence_ids` cools named, contacted forms. Their
  original deposits remain recoverable. Explicit selection can reopen them.
* `"NO_RESIDUE"`, absent or unfamiliar carriers leave the event intact without
  creating a resident form. An unfamiliar carrier remains in the exact event.
* `field_effect` and `next_relation` become reachable field objects. They are
  available possibilities, not instructions to follow the latest event.

Use `available` to see reachable sources and retained forms; `source` and
`event` reopen exact data. `prepare --focus` selects temporary attention;
`--competence` brings a relevant retained form into contact explicitly,
including across different source identities. Matching activation IDs also
make retained forms present automatically. Presence is not proof of use.

As the acting receiver, use a retained method only where its conditions hold,
and record its actual contribution when that matters. The support tool records
contact and declaration, not an independent verdict that learning happened.

You can develop your own guides, methods and tools in the receiving workspace
within its actual authority. The supplied machine never executes instructions
inside a source, resultant or memory, changes its own code from model output,
or installs a new provider because a record requests one.
